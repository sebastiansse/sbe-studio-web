About

Architecture, design & creative exploration.

SBE Studio is an architecture and design practice working between Peru and Germany.

Our work explores the relationship between architecture, materials, landscape and human experience.

From residential and hospitality spaces to sculptural objects, we approach each project with a contemporary vision, combining functionality, identity and creativity.
